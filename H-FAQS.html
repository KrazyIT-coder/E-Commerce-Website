What is KATHABI?

KATHABI is a clothing brand inspired by Filipino Culture, Creativity and traditional craftsmanship, combining cultural elements with modern fashion.

What products does KATHABI offer?

KATHABI offers clothing and fashion items inspired by Filipino heritage and contemporary styles.
