About Kathabí

Kathabí is a trendy clothing brand founded in the Philippines by a mere group of 5 college students in the year 2026. The company was originally founded in the hopes to introduce eco-friendly and Filipino cultural twists to pre-existing fashion aesthetics. These two goals aligned with the values of its founders, who knew the value of truly co-existing with the environment, alongside the value of being a citizen who took pride in their nationality. The company continues to follow emerging fashion trends over the years.

Focusing on producing eco-friendly and culturally accurate clothing and accessories, Kathabí’s full line of products is sure to fit every aesthetic tastes of people nationwide while giving their wardrobe a new sense of life. Kathabí continues to innovate, aiming to reduce waste consumption and ensure our products remain high quality for both older and younger generations.

The brand’s name reflects this mission: a fusion of "Katha" (to create) and "Habi" (to weave), symbolizing the weaving of story, culture, and sustainability into modern apparel.
